🌐 CAMPUSLOOP
A smart campus-sharing platform that helps students find, share, borrow, rent, and reuse useful resources within their college community.

🎯 The Challenge
Students often need books, calculators, tools, electronics, or academic equipment for a short time, but buying them can be costly. At the same time, many useful items remain unused.

💡 Our Approach
CampusLoop connects students who need something with those who already have it, making campus resources easier to access and reuse.

🤝 Verified student connections

📦 Borrow, rent, donate & share

📍 Campus-based availability

⭐ Trust scores & reviews

💬 Secure communication

🔔 Smart reminders & notifications
